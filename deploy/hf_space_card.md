# RetailMind

A retail operations console in one place: demand forecasting, procurement and supplier outreach,
shelf and loss-prevention alerts, a register with loyalty, warehouse planning, analytics with
profit and loss, a business assistant, and a customer app with a shopping list.

**It runs entirely in your browser.** The whole backend — database, sign-in, permissions, forecasting
— is part of the page, so there is no server to wait for and nothing leaves your device. Your
workspace is kept in this browser and survives reloads; Profile → Reset workspace starts it over.

Sign in from the landing page with any of the listed workspace accounts (the password is shown
on the sign-in screen).

- Source code and documentation: https://github.com/paulelisha500-ops/retailmind
- Also hosted on GitHub Pages: https://paulelisha500-ops.github.io/retailmind/
